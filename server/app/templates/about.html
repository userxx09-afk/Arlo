BCN: Barb's Camera Notifications

BCN keeps copies of the clips from a household's own Arlo, Blink and IP cameras on the household's own server, shows them on a private web page, lets you watch a camera live, and sends a notification the moment a camera sees motion.

It's a private, invite-only service run for family and friends. It isn't a public product and there's nothing to buy.

Signing in

People sign in with their Google account. The owner lets each person in once. After that, each person sees only the cameras they added themselves.
